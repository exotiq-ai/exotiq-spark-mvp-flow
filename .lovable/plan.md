# Rebuild Reel 1 First: Double-Booking

## Objective
Replace the failed reel with one cold-audience social ad that is understandable without prior knowledge of Exotiq or Rari. Do not reuse the current edit, crops, or single-screenshot structure.

## Creative reset
- Treat the viewer as an exotic rental operator who has never heard of Exotiq.
- Show the feared moment, the operator action, and the protected outcome—not a narrated feature list.
- Identify Rari clearly as Exotiq's AI fleet manager near the opening; never leave “I” unexplained.
- Make every product claim visible on screen while Rari says it.
- Keep the first frame active: real interface movement, pain hook, Rari speaking within 0.5 seconds. No logo intro or fade-in.

## Approval gates
### 1. Copy and storyboard
Prepare one revised script and a frame-by-frame storyboard for review before generating voice or editing video.

The storyboard will specify:
- exact Rari line;
- exact on-screen hook/payoff text;
- exact product action shown;
- duration and crop for each shot;
- what a cold viewer should understand from that beat.

No production starts until both copy and storyboard are approved.

### 2. Silent visual proof cut
Capture purpose-built footage and assemble a rough 9:16 cut without final voice or music. Review it muted to confirm the story is understandable from visuals and on-screen text alone.

No voice generation starts until the silent cut is approved.

### 3. Finished 9:16 reel
Generate each approved Rari line as a separate clip, add precise captions and a restrained music bed, then render the final vertical reel for review.

The 4:5 version is created only after the 9:16 version is approved.

## Planned story structure
1. **Pain, 0–2s:** Show two overlapping bookings for the same vehicle—not a generic fleet card. The hook names the conflict immediately.
2. **Context, 2–5s:** Establish that this is Exotiq fleet software and identify Rari as the AI fleet manager.
3. **Action, 5–9s:** Show the operator marking that exact vehicle Out of Service.
4. **Proof, 9–17s:** Cut through the resulting states: fleet status changes, calendar availability closes, and the Exotiq booking surface no longer offers the vehicle.
5. **Protection, 17–22s:** Return to the conflict and visibly remove the second booking path. Land the customer-facing benefit: no apology call or comped weekend.
6. **Payoff and CTA, 22–27s:** “One fleet. One truth.” followed by the approved comment CTA and a short `@exotiq_ai` identity lockup.

## Visual production rules
- Use multiple short screen recordings or UI states; never hold one screenshot for the full reel.
- Capture only populated, meaningful screens with readable dates, statuses, and vehicle identity.
- Compose desktop UI deliberately for portrait: full-width establishing view, then clean close-ups of one action. Never stretch a landscape screenshot to fill 9:16.
- Change the visual proof every 1–2 seconds through cuts, state changes, or purposeful punch-ins.
- Keep important text within Instagram safe zones; captions must remain readable on a phone.
- Hook text, captions, and payoff text remain separate layers and never compete.
- Keep the end card brief; product proof receives most of the runtime.

## Quality bar before delivery
- **Cold-viewer test:** someone unfamiliar with Exotiq can state the problem, action, and result after one muted viewing.
- **Claim-to-frame test:** every narrated claim has matching visible evidence.
- **Phone test:** interface labels and captions are readable at actual phone size.
- **First-second test:** frame one already contains the conflict and category context.
- **Crop test:** no key control, status, date, or vehicle identity is clipped.
- **Retention test:** no static visual beat lasts longer than two seconds without a meaningful state change.

- Do not alter the live application or production data.
- Do not rebuild Reels 2 and 3 until Reel 1 establishes an approved format.
