## Production sequence
1. Capture new, populated footage composed specifically for portrait; do not reuse the failed landscape crop.
2. Assemble a silent proof cut first. It must explain “car unavailable → second booking blocked” within two seconds and remain understandable muted.
3. After the silent cut is approved, generate the six separate Rari clips, sync captions, add a restrained ducked music bed, and render the finished 9:16 reel.
4. Create the independently laid-out 4:5 feed version only after the 9:16 reel is approved.

## Quality gates
- **Two-second test:** a cold viewer can identify a rental car, an unavailable state, and a blocked second booking by 0:02.
- **Claim-to-frame test:** every spoken product claim has matching real Exotiq proof on screen.
- **Phone test:** vehicle name, date, status, and disabled state remain legible at phone size.
- **Continuity test:** every shot uses the same Lamborghini and Saturday date.
- **Crop test:** no important control or label is clipped; landscape screenshots are not force-filled into portrait.
- **Retention test:** meaningful movement or proof changes at least every two seconds.
